Statement of Faith

What We Believe

We believe in one true God, eternally existing in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

We believe that God is the Creator of heaven and earth.

We believe in the deity of our Lord Jesus Christ. He was conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the Virgin Mary. He was crucified for the atonement of our sins. Through His suffering, we receive peace and healing.

On the third day, Jesus rose from the dead, ascended into heaven, and is now seated at the right hand of the Father. He will return to judge the world.

We believe that the Holy Spirit dwells within believers, empowering them to live holy lives and serve God.

We believe that the Bible is inspired by God and serves as the foundation of our faith and daily living.

We believe that salvation and justification come through faith in Jesus Christ alone. At the final judgment, both the saved and the lost will be resurrected, with eternal life for those who believe and eternal condemnation for those who do not.

We believe that the Church is the body of Christ. From the time of the apostles until today, believers belong to one body, called to manifest the glory of God.

Our Vision

A Glorious Church Filled with All the Fullness of God

"For with you is the fountain of life; in your light we see light."

Psalm 36:9

Our vision is to become a glorious church filled with all the fullness of God, experiencing His abundant love and reflecting His glory.

Biblical references: Psalm 36:7–9; Ephesians 1:23; 3:19; Colossians 2:9–10; John 10:10.

Our Mission

Living Out God's Calling

Our mission is grounded in the teachings of Jesus Christ and expressed through three commitments.

To Preach Good News

To proclaim good news to the poor, freedom for prisoners, recovery of sight for the blind, and freedom for the oppressed.

To proclaim the year of the Lord's favour.

Luke 4:18–19; Isaiah 61

To Make Disciples

To make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

To teach them to obey everything Jesus has commanded.

Matthew 28:19–20

To Prepare God's People

To equip God's people for works of service so that the body of Christ may be built up.

Ephesians 4:11–13
Our Core Values

What Guides Our Church

These ten core values guide our spiritual growth, worship, relationships, and service to God and others.

01

Love God Wholeheartedly

Love the Lord our God with all our heart, soul, mind, and strength.

Mark 12:30; John 21:15
02

Love Our Neighbours

Love our neighbours as ourselves and demonstrate Christ's love toward one another.

Mark 12:31; John 13:34–35
03

Pursue Christlikeness

Pursue becoming more like Christ as the goal of growth in our lives and church.

Galatians 4:19; Ephesians 4:13
04

Put God's Word into Practice

Apply God's Word faithfully in our daily lives.

2 Timothy 3:16–17; Matthew 7:24–27
05

Walk with the Holy Spirit

Keep in step with the Holy Spirit and rely on His guidance and power.

Zechariah 4:6; Galatians 5:25
06

Emphasize Prayer

Practise prayer, intercession, and thanksgiving in everything.

Isaiah 56:7; James 5:16
07

Cultivate True Worship

Develop a lifestyle of sincere worship in spirit and truth.

John 4:23–24; Psalm 150
08

Maintain Unity

Make every effort to preserve the unity of the Spirit within the body of Christ.

Ephesians 4:2–3; 1 Corinthians 12:12–27
09

Value All Generations

Encourage different generations to walk together in faith and serve God.

Malachi 4:6; Genesis 22:6–8
10

Serve Together in Ministry

Exercise team ministry, with every member contributing their gifts and fulfilling their part.

1 Peter 4:10–11; Ephesians 4:11–12
