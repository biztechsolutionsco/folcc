Discover Your Purpose

Why Am I Here?

Have you ever wondered what life is all about? Why were we born into this world? Is there more to life than what we experience every day?

Can we find lasting happiness and a deeper sense of purpose?

Understanding the purpose of our existence is an important step toward discovering the meaning of life and true happiness.

God's Love

There Is a God Who Loves You

The Bible teaches that God is good and that He created us to have a relationship with Him and enjoy His blessings.

God's love is enduring, and Jesus came so that we could experience the abundant life God desires for us.

God's everlasting love is described in Jeremiah 31:3.

Jeremiah 31:3

Jesus speaks about giving life in abundance.

John 10:10
Understanding Our World

However, There Is a Problem

God has given people the freedom to make choices, including choices between right and wrong.

Yet we live in a world affected by sin, suffering, and sickness. The Bible teaches that every person has sinned and fallen short of God's glory.

All people have sinned and fallen short of God's glory.

Romans 3:23

Sin damages our relationship with God and separates us from Him. Scripture also describes its ultimate consequence as death.

The consequence of sin is described in Romans 6:23.

Romans 6:23

Also see 1 John 1:8.

The Good News

What Is the Way Out?

Throughout history, people have sought ways to reconnect with God through religion, philosophy, and good works.

The Bible teaches that reconciliation with God comes through Jesus Christ.

Jesus identifies Himself as the way to the Father.

John 14:6

Jesus came into the world and gave His life to bear the consequences of human sin. Through Him, God offers forgiveness and eternal life.

Eternal life is God's gift through Jesus Christ.

Romans 6:23

Christ's sacrifice demonstrates God's love for us, even while we were still sinners.

See Romans 5:8.

Your Next Step

What Must I Do to Be Reconciled with God?

The original teaching presents three important responses: Admit, Believe, and Confess.

A

Admit

Acknowledge that you have sinned and need God's forgiveness. Turn away from sin in repentance.

B

Believe

Believe in your heart that Jesus Christ died for your sins and that He is alive today.

C

Confess

Confess Jesus Christ as Lord and invite Him into your life.

A Prayer of Faith

You May Pray a Simple Prayer

Dear Lord Jesus, I acknowledge that I have sinned and need Your forgiveness.

I believe that You died for my sins and rose again. I invite You into my life as my Saviour and Lord.

Thank You for Your forgiveness and the gift of eternal life. Guide me as I learn to follow You and become the person You want me to be.

Amen.

Remember: Romans 10:9 and John 1:12–13
Growing in Faith

Once We Are Saved, What Is the Purpose for Living?

The Christian life is an exciting journey of knowing God and becoming more like Jesus. There are five basic goals in the Christian life.

01

Worship

To know God and to love Him.

02

Discipleship

To develop a character that becomes more like Jesus.

03

Fellowship

To build meaningful relationships with other Christians.

04

Ministry

To use your gifts and talents to serve other people.

05

Evangelism

To share the good news with those who do not yet know Christ.

These five goals are nurtured through participation in the life of a local church. Reading the Bible, worshipping, and fellowshipping with other believers are important parts of growing in faith.

We encourage you to find a Bible-believing church community where you can worship regularly and continue your journey with Jesus Christ.
