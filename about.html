Our Story

Welcome to Fountain of Life Christian Church

Fountain of Life Christian Church was established in Toronto, 1998 with a vision to build a glorious church filled with all the fullness of God.

The main missions for the church is to preach good news to the poor; to go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything that Jesus has commanded us; to prepare God's people for works of service.

Fountain of Life Christian Church is a branch of Bread of Life Christian Church Taipei and is part of the BOL Global Apostolic Network.

Our Mission

Living Out Our Faith

Our church's mission is rooted in the teachings of Jesus Christ and expressed through three important commitments.

Share the Good News

To proclaim the good news to the poor, freedom for the oppressed, and the hope of God's salvation.

Luke 4:18–19

Make Disciples

To make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, and teaching them to follow Jesus.

Matthew 28:19–20

Equip God's People

To prepare and equip believers for works of service, building up the body of Christ.

Ephesians 4:11–13
